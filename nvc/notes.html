Küsi endalt vaikselt: Millist vajadust see inimene püüab rahuldada sellega, mida ta just ütles? Isegi kui edastamine on kehv, on peaaegu alati alltaga legitiimne vajadus. Selle leidmine annab sulle millele vastata — mitte ainult pinnale reageerida.

Empathy for the other sideEmpaatia teise poole suhtes
You don't have to agree — but try to understandSa ei pea nõustuma — aga proovi mõista
NVC distinguishes empathy from agreement. You can say "I understand why you experienced it that way" without saying "and therefore you were right."

For the staff side: they created something they believed was for students. Having it met with a sharp public email genuinely hurt. That is real, even if the students' frustration is also real.

For the student side: they asked for something reasonable — clarity and fairness — and the response focused on their tone, not their substance. That is also real.

Holding both without collapsing them into one is the core skill of the day. NVC eristab empaatiat nõustumisest. Sa saad öelda "Mõistan, miks sa seda nii kogesid" ilma ütlemata "ja seega olid sa õige."

Personali poole jaoks: nad lõid midagi, mida uskusid tudengite jaoks olevat. Selle kohtamine terava avaliku kirjaga tegi tõesti haiget. See on tõeline, isegi kui tudengite frustratsioon on ka tõeline.

Tudengite poole jaoks: nad palusid midagi mõistlikku — selgust ja õiglust — ja vastus keskendus nende toonile, mitte sisule. See on ka tõeline.

After the meetingPärast koosolekut
Give yourself some graceOle enda vastu lahke
You will not do this perfectly. Nobody does NVC perfectly in a live conflict — least of all when you have your own stake in the outcome. That is not the goal.

The goal is to stay a little more curious and a little less reactive than you might otherwise be. Even one moment of genuine listening — one question instead of a statement, one pause before reacting — changes the temperature of a room.

If the meeting goes well, it's because everyone tried. If it gets difficult, it's not your failure — it means the underlying wounds are real and need time. That is useful information too.
